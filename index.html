About Vusani Ikhaya Properties

Trusted Rooms. Real People. Real Value.

Established in May 2026, Vusani Ikhaya Properties is a South African property business focused on connecting people with accommodation and property opportunities.

We aim to make finding a room, renting a home, buying a property or marketing a property easier through accessible listings, reliable communication and a professional service.

Our initial focus is Alexandra Township and surrounding Johannesburg communities, including East Bank, Tsutsumane, River Park, Wynberg, Marlboro, Bramley and Lombardy East, with plans to expand our reach.

Our Mission

To connect tenants, landlords, buyers and sellers through a convenient and transparent property platform.

Our Values

  • Trust and honest communication.
  • Professional and respectful service.
  • Transparency about listings and fees.
  • Accessible property information.
  • Responsible handling of customer information.
